The problem

A crushing plant runs blind
between shift reports.

2,500 t/h of ROM feed moves through crushers, screens and a wet desands circuit — but the numbers that matter arrive in a spreadsheet hours after the tonnes are already lost.

01

02

03

The shift

Model intelligence isn't the limiting factor. Live, trusted context is.

The plant already emits ~450 readings a second across 151 instruments. The win isn't a bigger model — it's making that stream live, governed, and answerable in one place.

What the plant needs from its data

Context, control, cost, choice.

01

Context

151 live tags, the flowsheet topology, and a semantic layer an agent can actually reason over.

02

Control

Every table and model governed in Unity Catalog, with change history captured automatically.

03

Cost

Serverless compute that scales to zero between shifts — you pay for tonnes, not idle clusters.

04

Choice

Open Delta and Iceberg formats. No lock-in on your data, your model, or your cloud.
