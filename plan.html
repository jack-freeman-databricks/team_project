The call

Split it. Three laptops, not one.

One laptop makes two of us spectators. The work decomposes into three tiers that touch at exactly two seams. The catch is that the pipeline is a linear chain, so a naive split leaves two people idle until lunch. Two devices fix that, and they are the whole reason this plan works.

The mechanism

What we are building

Synthetic plant data lands through a streaming pipeline into Lakebase, changes flow back into Unity Catalog, an analytical layer computes the mass balance, a model scores vibration, and an app plus an AI layer sit on top.

The split

Three lanes

Each lane owns its own Unity Catalog schemas, prefixes its pipelines and jobs with its own letter, and has a scratch schema for experiments. That is what keeps three parallel Claude sessions from overwriting each other.

Getting going

The contract is already written

Everything the three lanes need to agree on is committed. The tag register is generated rather than hand-maintained, and the generator refuses to emit the CSVs unless the design mass balance closes at every unit node and the wet and dry sides of the desands circuit reconcile. That check has already earned its keep twice: it caught a secondary crusher discharging more than it received, and a twelve tonne per hour gap between the two measurement methods.

Read contract/naming.md first, run phase 0 together from prompts/00-phase-0-together.md, then take a lane and paste your own prompt into your own session.
