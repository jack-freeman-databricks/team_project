What the agent decided, not just typed
--------------------------------------
The change was not dictated. The agent identified that the operational schema
was a flat pair of tables with zero foreign keys and no free-text column, which
failed two of the Build 1 requirements at once, and proposed work_order plus
work_order_note as a 1:N model that fixes both while also giving Lakebase Search
something real to index. Migration 003 then followed from discovering that
lakebase_text exposes BM25 ranking and lakebase_vector exposes ANN index
methods, so the search had to be built as two indexes fused rather than one.
