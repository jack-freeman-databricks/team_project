Representative business question for Ironbark Resources
=======================================================

"Which crushing and screening assets are costing us the most unplanned downtime,
 what failure modes are driving it, how does their measured vibration compare
 against the governed ISO 10816 alarm limits, and is anything still open?"

Why this is the question a maintenance planner actually asks:

Ironbark's constraint is not detecting a single alarm, it is deciding where to
spend a limited maintenance window. That decision needs three things joined
together, and they normally live in three different systems:

  - unplanned downtime and its cause, which sits in the work order system
  - the engineering limits for each instrument, which are governed reference
    data owned by the reliability team
  - the free-text findings from the fitters who did the work

Answering it requires the governed Unity Catalog tag register (synced into
Lakebase as dim_tag_online, read-only) to be joined against writable
operational Postgres tables (work_order, work_order_note) at low latency, from
the application, without the data leaving the customer's account.
