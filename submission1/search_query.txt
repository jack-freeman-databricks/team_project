Lakebase Search: hybrid vector plus full-text over operator notes
=================================================================

Natural-language query:

  "bearing starting to fail on a crusher, what did we find and what did we fit"

Why hybrid rather than either half alone:

  full text (BM25, lakebase_text)   catches exact domain terms an operator types:
                                    "outer race", a tag id, a part number
  vector (ANN cosine, lakebase_vector) catches meaning: the query says "starting
                                    to fail", the note says "play in the outer
                                    race by hand" and never uses the word fail

The two ranked lists are combined with reciprocal rank fusion, so a record that
scores well on either signal surfaces, and one that scores well on both wins.

Embeddings: databricks-gte-large-en (1024 dimensions), an in-account Databricks
serving endpoint. Note text is never sent outside the customer's account.

Indexes:
  ix_bm25_note  USING lakebase_bm25 (note_tsv tsvector_bm25_ops)
  ix_ann_note   USING lakebase_ann  (note_embedding vector_cosine_ops)
